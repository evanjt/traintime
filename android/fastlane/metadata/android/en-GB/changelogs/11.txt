TrainTime is now on Android! Also on Garmin, Apple Watch and iOS.

- Share a trip from SBB Mobile into TrainTime and it picks up your train. Later legs are saved as a route you can open any time, with a reminder before departure.
- Save any departure as a reminder from the board.
- Route reminders now allow for your walk to the station, so you leave on time.
- Your Wear OS or Garmin watch mirrors what the phone is tracking and keeps favourites in sync.
- Duplicate departures are gone.
