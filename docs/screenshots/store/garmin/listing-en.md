# Connect IQ listing, English

Your wrist has the internet and precise GPS. Swiss timetables are accurate to the minute. TrainTime connects the two: live SBB train, bus and tram departures around you, with platform, delay and walking distance, tracking whether you'll make it with every step. No more missing your train while you dig out your phone. Glance and go.

NEARBY
The closest stations to you, found by GPS. No timetable searching. Each departure shows destination, line, platform, delay and direction of travel.

TRACK
Select a departure to follow it. A live countdown against your walk to the station tells you whether you'll make it. The train formation diagram shows where to stand on the platform.

MY STATIONS
Star a connection and it rides above the gold line at every station. Pin your home station to keep it first.

PHONE LINK
Pairs with the TrainTime app on iPhone or Android through Garmin Connect. Send a departure from the phone to the watch, queue reminders from the wrist, and the phone shows live what the watch is tracking.

MODES
Trains, buses, trams, boats, funiculars and cable cars. Up to 5 nearby stations per mode.

SWITZERLAND-WIDE
Real-time timetable data from opentransportdata.swiss: SBB (CFF/FFS), PostBus and regional operators, 51,000+ stations.

PRIVACY
No account, no tracking, no analytics. Your coordinates are processed in-memory and immediately discarded, never stored or shared. Full policy at traintime.ch/privacy.

OPEN SOURCE
Both the Train Time app and its API are open source.
