BACKGROUND LOCATION (UIBackgroundModes: location)

What it is for
TrainTime tracks one departure at a time. While a tracking session is running,
the app shows a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with a
countdown to the train, the platform, the live delay, and the time the user has
to leave to make the walk to the platform. That leave-by time is derived from
the user's distance to the station, so it is only correct if location keeps
updating once the app is backgrounded. That is the sole use of the background
mode.

When it runs
Only while the user is actively tracking a departure. It starts when a departure
is tapped and stops the moment tracking stops, the train departs, or the user
taps Stop on the Live Activity. It is never on at idle, and the app has no
always-on or periodic background location behaviour.

Power use
Location accuracy is tiered by how far away the departure is. Beyond 30 minutes
location is off entirely (kCLLocationAccuracyKilometer, 2 km distance filter);
it moves to balanced inside 30 minutes and to high accuracy inside 10. A trip
several hours away costs effectively nothing.

Disclosure and user control
- On first open after updating, a one-step onboarding page explains that
  tracking continues in the background, that location is used to do it, and that
  it can be turned off. New installs see the same page in sequence, immediately
  after the step that introduces tracking.
- A disclosure alert ("TrainTime uses background location") always precedes the
  system Always-location prompt. The app never requests Always authorisation
  without it, including on a background relaunch.
- Settings > Notifications > "Keep tracking in the background" turns the whole
  behaviour off. Turning it off is confirmed with an alert explaining that
  tracking will then only run while the app is open.
- The location indicator stays visible for the whole session.

Degraded behaviour when refused
Refusing background or Always location does not disable any feature. Tracking,
the countdown, the delay, the platform and the leave alert all keep working; the
walk margin falls back to the last known position. This path is covered by unit
tests.

How to reproduce
1. Open the app and allow location. The nearby board loads (Swiss stations only,
   so a Swiss location is needed; see the demo location below).
2. Tap any departure to start tracking.
3. Background the app. The Live Activity appears on the Lock Screen with the
   countdown and the leave-by time.
4. Tap Stop on the Live Activity, or reopen and leave the tracking screen. The
   session and background location both end.

Demo location (the app only covers Switzerland)
Bern Bahnhof: 46.9480, 7.4474. In the simulator, Features > Location > Custom.

Data handling
Coordinates are sent to api.traintime.ch only to find nearby stations, processed
in memory, and discarded. Nothing is stored server-side or shared with third
parties. No account, no analytics. Policy: https://traintime.ch/privacy
