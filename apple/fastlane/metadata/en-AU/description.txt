TrainTime puts Swiss public transport departures right on your wrist, and now in your pocket. See what's leaving from nearby train, tram and bus stops, ferries, cable cars and funiculars, with real-time delays, platform numbers and walking distance. Haptic feedback when you're running behind keeps you from missing a departure.

NEARBY DEPARTURES
TrainTime uses your location to find the closest Swiss stations and shows upcoming departures with destination, platform, delay and direction of travel.

FOCUSED TRACKING
Tap a departure to track it. A live countdown shows your remaining time based on your walking distance to the station, so you know whether you'll make it. See the full train formation diagram so you know exactly where to stand on the platform.

iPHONE + APPLE WATCH
Browse departures and track trains from your iPhone with the full companion app, or glance at your Watch for the essentials. Send a tracked departure from your phone to your Watch with one tap. Add the home screen widget for instant access.

MULTIPLE TRANSPORT MODES
Switch between trains, buses, trams, boats, funiculars and cable cars. Browse up to 5 nearby stations per mode across 4 transport types. TrainTime only shows what's around you.

WORKS ACROSS SWITZERLAND
Powered by real-time data from opentransportdata.swiss, covering 51,000+ stations across the entire country.

PRIVACY FIRST
No account needed. No tracking. No analytics. Your coordinates are processed in-memory by our API and immediately discarded, never stored or shared. Full privacy policy at traintime.ch/privacy.

OPEN SOURCE
Both the app and API are open source.

This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by SBB or any Swiss transit operator. Departure data is sourced from opentransportdata.swiss and may not always be accurate or up to date.
