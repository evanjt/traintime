Big update!

- Share a trip from SBB Mobile straight into TrainTime, and it picks up your train. Later trips are saved as a route you can open any time, with a reminder before departure.
- Save any departure as a reminder straight from the board.
- Route reminders now allow for your walk to the station, so you leave on time. Set how early to be warned in Settings.
- Open a saved route and tap any connection to start tracking it.
- Watch integration! Your Apple or Garmin Watch now mirrors what the phone is tracking, shows when it is live, and launches from the phone, sends a saved route to it from the reminder, and your favourites stay in sync.
- A quick walkthrough introduces the app on first launch.
- Choose a light, dark or system appearance in Settings.
- Duplicate departures no longer appear in the list.

Please rate TrainTime from Settings if you like it.
